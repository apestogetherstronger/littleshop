# Imported recipe data

The recipe records in `recipes/` derive from **UniTools — theunitools.com**, https://theunitools.com/en/data, licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . The full license is in `licenses/unitools-CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`.

These adaptations (English text selection, ingredient IDs, unit normalization and validation metadata) are distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license. Photos are excluded. This content license applies to this imported data, not to the surrounding application code. Each record preserves its source slug, dataset revision, checksum, attribution and change notice.

Source dietary tags are not independently trustworthy: some conflict with animal ingredients. Such entries remain `needs_review`. “Ready” means structurally usable with mapped ingredients, not culinary testing, nutritional verification, guaranteed supermarket availability, or product-level dietary certification.

The source's original batch servings and instructions are retained; quantities are not automatically rescaled. Check the displayed servings before cooking. Pantry seasonings and water are listed separately and excluded from basket estimates. Count-based produce and spoon measures may remain unpriced by supermarket feeds; they are never silently treated as free.
