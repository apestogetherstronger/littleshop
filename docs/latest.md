### Day 3: Ratatouille (65 min)

Serves: 4 · vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free

Recipe: UniTools — theunitools.com · CC-BY-SA-4.0 · [Source](https://theunitools.com/en/data) · [Full recipe record](../data/recipes/import_f3e71d60f05afb07.json)

Pantry check (excluded from basket estimates): 3 sprig Thyme; to taste toTaste Salt and pepper

Method:

1. Fry the aubergine over high heat in two spoons of oil until the edges brown, then set it aside. Aubergine drinks oil instantly — do not add more, it gives it back later.
2. In the same pan, fry the courgette until golden, about four minutes, and add it to the aubergine. It should stay firm.
3. Fry the pepper until soft with a few charred spots and set it aside as well.
4. In the remaining oil, sweat the onion until soft, add the garlic and thyme and give it half a minute, until fragrant.
5. Add the tomatoes and cook them down over medium heat into a thick sauce, about twenty minutes. It should hold on a spoon rather than run off it.
6. Return the vegetables to the pan, season and warm them through together for ten minutes over low heat. Take it off, add the basil and let it stand — ratatouille is better warm than hot.

### Day 4: Ghapama (120 min)

Serves: 6 · vegetarian, gluten-free

Recipe: UniTools — theunitools.com · CC-BY-SA-4.0 · [Source](https://theunitools.com/en/data) · [Full recipe record](../data/recipes/import_2a9f8a4decc4d1fd.json)

Pantry check (excluded from basket estimates): 1 tsp Cinnamon; to taste toTaste Salt

Method:

1. Cut the top off the pumpkin with its stalk; that is the lid. Scoop out the seeds and loose flesh with a spoon, leaving a wall two fingers thick.
2. Boil the rice in salted water for seven minutes, to half done, and drain.
3. Cover the dried fruit with boiling water for ten minutes, drain, and chop the apricots and prunes coarsely. Chop the nuts.
4. Mix the rice with the fruit, nuts, melted butter, honey and cinnamon. Salt it: a sweet filling without salt tastes flat.
5. Fill the pumpkin, without packing it down, put the cut lid back on and set it on a baking tray.
6. Bake at 180 °C for an hour and a half. Test at the side with a knife: it should go into the wall without resistance.
7. Rest fifteen minutes, carry it out whole and cut it into wedges from the crown to the base.
