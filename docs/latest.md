## v1 interpretation notes

- Cost estimates round up to full packs when pack sizes are known. Ambiguous multi-buy, group and conditional offers use shelf prices instead. Unpriced ingredients are excluded from totals, not counted as free.
- Product matching is deterministic substring matching using aliases in config/ingredients.yml; adjust aliases when a supermarket uses unexpected wording.
- Dietary filtering is recipe-tag based. Product-level allergen and kosher certification checking is not implemented in v1.
